What the Studio does

  1. Read the source. Identify the real product, pieces, alternative views and conflicting swatches.
  2. Protect identity. Record count, form, material appearance, motifs and state. Originals stay authoritative.
  3. Compose for use. A sophisticated tabletop or dining setting with a counted arrangement and a motif close-up.
  4. Choose relevant support. Source-backed Midas pieces when appropriate; scene context when no suitable product reference exists.
  5. Create selected views. Hero, wider composition and source-visible detail; optional white, angle, style and lighting series.
  6. Review and correct. Apply the checks below to the actual file; repair the specific defect and retain previous versions.
  7. Deliver. Images, source links, selected brief, recorded assessments and known/unknown specifications.

Produce one finished, extremely photorealistic home, office or outdoor product image. Do not ask the client to write these observations. The first furniture references are authoritative; do not replace them with a similar collection.
